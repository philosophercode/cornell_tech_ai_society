About

Cornell Tech AI Society is a student-run club at Cornell University's NYC campus. It's a space for students of every background to explore AI — its applications, theories, and impact on daily life and society — with a focus on interdisciplinary exchange and engagement with leaders at the frontier of AI.

  • 🎤 Talks & panels

    Conversations with researchers, builders, and thought leaders.

  • 💻 Hands-on workshops

    Build real projects with AI tools, with No-Code Works.

  • 🤔 Ethics & philosophy

    Discussions on implications, risks, and governance.

  • 🎨 Creative galleries

    Student work at the intersection of AI and art.

  • 🚀 Hackathons

    Team up to ship useful or provocative projects.

  • 📚 Past session

    AI Agents from First Principles at EmpireHacks 2026.
